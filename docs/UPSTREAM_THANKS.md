**Title**

Thank you for steam_monitor — sharing a Windows tray companion

**Message**

Hi @misiektoja,

Thank you for creating and maintaining steam_monitor, including its setup wizard and documentation.

I built a small Windows companion called [SteamMonitorTray](https://github.com/Jazzwind/SteamMonitorTray). It launches the existing steam_monitor command in real PowerShell sessions and adds tray controls, multiple-profile management, Windows startup integration and a multilingual interface. Monitoring remains handled by your project, and stopping uses its normal Ctrl+C handling.

The README prominently links to steam_monitor and credits you and its contributors. I wanted to share the companion and say thanks for the work behind the monitoring engine.

No action is needed on your side. Thank you again!
